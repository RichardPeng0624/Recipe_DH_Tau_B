for the measured inter-night barycentric drift -- confirm which literal prior
the quoted job used against its own script before finalizing this value.}
\tablenotetext{b}{The absolute-flux-calibrated benchmark series (007PM-EQ-GP)
instead retrieves $\log M/M_{\rm Jup} \sim \mathcal{N}(1.079, 0.145)$ and
$\log R/R_{\rm Jup} \sim \mathcal{N}(0.415, 0.100)$ (both from Xuan et al.\ 2024
Table~1 evolutionary-model priors), with $\log g$ derived via $g=GM/R^2$ rather
than fitted directly; $\log R$ additionally sets the $(R/d)^2$ absolute-flux
normalisation, $d=135.2$~pc (Gaia DR3). Use the $\log g$-direct
parameterisation shown above only for the per-chip-median-normalised
(``mednorm'') or Ruffio-likelihood series, which carry no absolute flux
information and so cannot separately constrain $M$ and $R$.}
\tablenotetext{}{The equilibrium-chemistry opacity list is H$_2$O, $^{12}$CO,
$^{13}$CO, CH$_4$, NH$_3$, H$_2$S, HCN, CO$_2$, FeH, HF (10 species; $^{13}$CO
derived from $^{12}$CO via the ratio parameter above, HF derived from
$[{\rm F/H}]$). Na and Ca were retrieved as two additional direct log-VMR
parameters, $\log X_{\rm Na}\sim\mathcal{U}(-10,-2)$ and
$\log X_{\rm Ca}\sim\mathcal{U}(-10,-2)$, in early analysis but were dropped
from the adopted model on 2026-07-07 after an explicit removal test showed no
parameter shifted by more than $1\sigma$ and the Bayesian evidence was
unaffected within its uncertainty; the completed absolute-flux benchmark run
was not re-executed after this simplification (see
\texttt{retrieval\_framework\_notes.md} \S~7 for the exact reconciliation
needed before quoting that specific job's parameter count in the paper).}
